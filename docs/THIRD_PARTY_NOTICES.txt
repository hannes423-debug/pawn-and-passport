3D CHESS SET  (assets/board3d/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Chess-4K-MaterialTest" (https://skfb.ly/oBRqS) by chiwei is licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The model and its textures have been modified for real-time web use: the pieces
were reduced to one canonical mesh per type and decimated from 774,000 to about
13,000 triangles, and the 4K textures were resized to 1K WebP with occlusion,
roughness and metalness packed into a single image. The normal maps were
dropped, having been measured as flat. See docs/LICENSE_TRACKER.md.
